Smokies Grand Loop

Jerome's seven-day motorcycle camping loop through the Blue Ridge and the Smokies on a BMW G310 GS. Two free forest camps, every road on the list, and one pass on the Dragon.

The plan: fly in at Triple Tree, ride up into the mountains, base at two free national-forest camps, and ride the famous roads unloaded on day loops. Each day below has the route, a sketch map, and a button that opens turn-by-turn directions.

Camps

Wash Creek, on Yellow Gap Rd in Pisgah. Free dispersed sites near North Mills River. Nights 1, 5 and 6.

Lake Santeetlah, on Pine Ridge Rd north of Robbinsville off US 129. Free dispersed sites marked with brown numbered posts. Nights 2 to 4.

  1. 1

    Triple Tree to Wash Creek

    About 110 miles, 3 hours riding. No interstate.

    SC 11US 276

    Leave Triple Tree and stop at Cycle Gear in Greenville. Pick up SC 11, then take US 276 up the escarpment past Caesars Head. Continue through Brevard to Wash Creek.

    Cycle Gear opens at 10 AM on Sundays. The address is 2017 Wade Hampton Blvd.

    • Cycle Gear, Greenville for bike pads, first stop of the trip.
    • SC 11, the Cherokee Foothills Scenic Highway, along the base of the escarpment.
    • US 276 up Caesars Head: tight switchbacks and a big overlook at the top.
    • Through Brevard, then into Pisgah National Forest.

    Tonight: Wash Creek, a free dispersed site on Yellow Gap Rd. Jerome camped here on a past trip.

  2. 2

    Blue Ridge Parkway to Santeetlah

    About 135 miles, 4 hours riding.

    Blue Ridge PkwyUS 19/74US 129

    Ride the Parkway south along its highest stretch, past Richland Balsam, all the way to its end in Cherokee. Then go through Bryson City and the Nantahala Gorge, on to Robbinsville, and set up camp at Lake Santeetlah.

    Check before you go: look at the NPS road status page for closures around Devil's Courthouse (MP 422). If it's closed, detour through Waynesville and Maggie Valley and rejoin the Parkway at Soco Gap.

    • Blue Ridge Parkway from US 276 south, the highest section of the whole Parkway.
    • Richland Balsam, the Parkway's highest point at 6,053 ft.
    • Parkway ends in Cherokee, then US 19/74 through the Nantahala Gorge past the rafting put-ins.
    • Fuel and supplies in Robbinsville before heading to the lake.

    Tonight: Lake Santeetlah, free dispersed sites on Pine Ridge Rd. Home base for three nights.

  3. 3

    Cherohala out and back

    About 110 miles, 3.5 hours riding. Unloaded bike.

    Cherohala SkywayRiver Rd

    Ride the Skyway west to Tellico Plains and have lunch in town. Then take River Rd to Bald River Falls, which you can see right from the road. Ride back over the Skyway to camp.

    This is the one road you ride both ways, and it's just as good going east.

    • Cherohala Skyway: 43 miles, no towns, no gas, up to 5,400 ft. Fill up in Robbinsville.
    • Lunch in Tellico Plains.
    • Bald River Falls, a 90-ft waterfall visible right from the bridge on River Rd.
    • Skyway again on the way back, with the afternoon light the other way.

    Tonight: Back at Lake Santeetlah. Tent stays pitched all day.

  4. 4

    The Dragon and the Smokies

    About 170 miles, 5.5 hours riding. This is the big day, so start early.

    US 129 DragonFoothills PkwyUS 321US 441NC 143

    Fuel up at Deals Gap, then ride the Dragon north. That's your only pass. Pick up the Foothills Parkway to Walland, then 321 to Townsend. Take Little River Rd to Sugarlands, then 441 south over Newfound Gap to Cherokee. Head back through Bryson City and NC 143 to camp.

    In the Smokies you need a parking tag if you park longer than 15 minutes.

    • Tail of the Dragon: 318 curves in 11 miles. Ridden once, northbound, on an unloaded bike.
    • Foothills Parkway, a quiet ridge-top road with views back over the Smokies.
    • Townsend, the quiet side of the Smokies, then Little River Rd along the water.
    • Newfound Gap on US 441: over 5,000 ft, crossing back into North Carolina on the state line.
    • Loop closes through Cherokee, Bryson City and NC 143.

    Tonight: Back at Lake Santeetlah. Last night at the lake.

  5. 5

    Moonshiner 28 back to Wash Creek

    About 170 miles, 5.5 hours riding. Break camp.

    NC 28US 64

    Start at Deals Gap and ride 28 past Fontana and Stecoah to Franklin. Then go through the Cullasaja Gorge to Highlands, and on through Cashiers and down US 64 to Brevard and Wash Creek.

    Cullasaja Falls only has a small roadside pull-off, so watch for it.

    • Moonshiner 28, the old bootlegging road along Fontana Lake.
    • Fontana Dam, the tallest dam in the eastern US, right beside the road.
    • Cullasaja Gorge on US 64: the road is cut into the cliff, with Dry Falls and Bridal Veil Falls near Highlands.
    • Highlands and Cashiers, then down the escarpment to Brevard.

    Tonight: Wash Creek again. Loaded bike all day, so an easier pace.

At camp

Leave at camp: your tent, sleeping bag, and pad. Stake and guy the tent well in case of afternoon storms.
Take with you or lock up: food, toiletries, trash, and anything scented, because this is bear country. Also take your documents, electronics, and tools.
Dispersed rules: sites are free and first come, first served. Pack out everything you bring in.
Mileage and times are rough riding estimates and don't include stops. Sketch maps show the shape of each day, not exact roads. Live location comes from Glympse and only updates while Jerome is sharing.
